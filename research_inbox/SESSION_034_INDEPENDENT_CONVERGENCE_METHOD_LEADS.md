## 1. Core methodological proposal

The input proposes studying **independent convergence**:

```text
separate reports
        +
shared low-frequency feature cluster
        +
limited exposure or contamination
        +
close temporal, geographic, or structural fit
        ↓
priority for investigation
```

This does not establish that the witnesses' interpretation is correct or that the event was extraordinary.

The central distinction is:

```text
raw observation
        ≠
observer interpretation
        ≠
researcher explanation
```

Example:

```text
observation:
orange oval; silent; stopped; tilted; accelerated northeast

interpretation:
alien craft / drone / secret technology / spiritual event
```

## 2. Fields proposed by the input

### Event and source

- date and time, preferably normalized to UTC;
- place and coordinates;
- original wording;
- first report/publication date;
- source type;
- sensor or media attachments.

### Observer and report quality

- firsthand or retold;
- recorded immediately or retrospectively;
- observer condition and expertise;
- single or multiple witnesses;
- investigator prompting;
- prior exposure to similar stories.

### Observation features

- shape;
- color and luminosity;
- motion;
- sound;
- duration;
- direction;
- altitude/distance estimate;
- environmental effects;
- instrument corroboration.

### Contamination and independence

- direct contact between reporters;
- shared investigator;
- shared local rumor;
- news, social-media, film, fiction, or folklore exposure;
- whether statements were recorded before cross-exposure;
- whether reports derive from one hidden source.

### Alternative causes

- aircraft;
- satellite or reentry;
- meteor;
- balloon or drone;
- atmospheric optics;
- astronomical object;
- shared environmental trigger;
- common perceptual or linguistic structure.

## 5. Important boundaries

- Multiple reports can describe one ordinary event.
- Multiple independent reports can reflect one shared environmental cause.
- Similar descriptions may arise from human perception, language, genre, or investigator prompting.
- Report independence is not proved merely by geographic distance.
- A shared interpretation is weaker evidence than a shared low-level observation feature.
- A high convergence score is not a truth score.
- Inability to identify a mundane cause is not evidence for a preferred extraordinary cause.
- Modern terminology must not be projected backward without documenting the translation.
- `UGO` remains undefined unless a source or project definition specifies it.
