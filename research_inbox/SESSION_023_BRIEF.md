# SESSION_023 — Acquisition Brief

**Focus:** The 1948–1979 Shaver/Palmer transmission gap  
**Primary research question:** RQ-023  
**Status:** READY FOR ACQUISITION

## Objective

Identify the actual carriers—people, publications, clubs, correspondence networks, archives, conventions, and media—that preserved, transformed, or reintroduced Shaver/Palmer underground motifs between 1948 and 1979.

This is not a request to prove a single continuous lineage. The correct result may be several parallel carrier networks, a broken chain, or no documented bridge at all.

## Required research waves

### Wave 1 — Richard Shaver after Amazing Stories

Find dated primary or near-primary evidence for:

- Shaver's publications, newsletters, clubs, correspondence, lectures, artwork, and rock-book activity after 1948.
- Reprints and republications of Shaver material.
- Editors, publishers, or clubs that kept his work circulating.
- Reader organizations and mailing lists.

### Wave 2 — Raymond Palmer's post-1948 network

Trace:

- Fate, Other Worlds, Flying Saucers, Search, The Hidden World, and related imprints.
- Repeated authors, advertisers, letter writers, club organizers, and distributors.
- Explicit reuse of Shaver concepts or terminology.
- Palmer's connections to Barker, Bender, Crabb, Bernard, and other hollow-earth/UFO publishers.

### Wave 3 — Fanzine and mail-order ecology

Acquire evidence from:

- Saucerian publications.
- Borderland Sciences material.
- Hollow-earth newsletters and catalogs.
- Convention programs and membership rosters.
- Advertisements, book lists, correspondence columns, and reprint notices.

### Wave 4 — Possible entry into the Bennewitz-era network

Search for documented reading, possession, citation, contact, or shared networks involving:

- Paul Bennewitz.
- Richard Doty.
- William Moore.
- John Lear.
- Linda Moulton Howe.
- Bruce Walton / Branton.
- Tal Levesque / Jason Bishop III.

## Output requirements

For every source or claim, provide:

1. Exact title, author/editor, publisher, date, volume/issue, and pages.
2. Stable URL, archive identifier, catalog record, or repository location.
3. Exact quotation under 25 words when legally and practically available.
4. What the source proves—and what it does not prove.
5. Proposed edge type:
   - DIRECT_CITATION
   - KNOWN_COPY
   - SHARED_PUBLISHER
   - PERSONAL_CONTACT
   - DOCUMENTED_INFLUENCE
   - MASS_MEDIA_EXPOSURE
   - SHARED_MOTIF
   - SPECULATIVE_SIMILARITY
   - NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE
   - UNKNOWN
6. Evidence class A–E.
7. Alternative explanations.
8. New entities, publications, chronology events, propagation nodes, and research questions.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not infer transmission from similarity, shared era, or membership in the general UFO community. Do not use a modern article as proof of what a 1950s source contained unless the original source is independently located. Preserve conflicting dates and names rather than silently resolving them.

## Suggested acquisition summary

Close the response using `schemas/ACQUISITION_SUMMARY.md`, but count only items actually supported by cited evidence.
