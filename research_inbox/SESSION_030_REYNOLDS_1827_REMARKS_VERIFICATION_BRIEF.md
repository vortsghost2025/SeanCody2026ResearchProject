## Original objective

Verify or falsify the repeatedly cited claim that Jeremiah N. Reynolds published a separate 1827 work titled approximately:

> *Remarks of Symmes' Theory Which Appeared in the American Quarterly Review*

## Resolved

- The object is not treated as a bibliographic ghost.
- The LOC catalogue attributes it to Jeremiah N. Reynolds.
- The common title using `Remarks of Symmes' Theory` is incorrect.
- The pamphlet postdates both the reported 1825 Reynolds–Symmes break and Adams's November 4, 1826 pivot description.
- Reynolds remained publicly engaged with a review of Symmes's theory in 1827.

## Not resolved

- No scan or transcription of the pamphlet was acquired.
- Reynolds's doctrinal position is unknown.
- The matching *American Quarterly Review* article has not yet been verified from exact page images.
- The project cannot yet classify the pamphlet as defense, qualification, modification, strategic reframing, or rejection.
