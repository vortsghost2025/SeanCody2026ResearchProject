# Research Inbox

This directory is the landing zone for unreviewed research acquisitions.

## Rules

- Files here are **not canonical**.
- Preserve the original research output exactly when practical.
- One acquisition batch per file.
- Recommended naming: `SESSION_###_<TOPIC>_ACQUISITION.md`.
- Each file should include source citations, page numbers, proposed record IDs, uncertainties, and follow-up tasks.
- After review, material is routed to canonical records, quarantine, negative-evidence records, source registries, or research questions.

## Intake states

- `NEW`
- `UNDER_REVIEW`
- `PARTIALLY_INGESTED`
- `INGESTED`
- `QUARANTINED`
- `SUPERSEDED`

The inbox preserves acquisition history even after records are normalized elsewhere.
