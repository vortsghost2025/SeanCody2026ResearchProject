## 1. Overall judgment

The package is a productive lead map. It is not an import-ready dataset.

Its strongest contribution is not a completed Grey or reptilian lineage. It is the identification of two narrow research problems:

1. **Grey visual standardization and contamination** — how fiction, hypnosis, illustration, television, film, publishing, and later testimony interact;
2. **modern reptilian synthesis and Grey/Reptilian fusion** — where distinct occult, weird-fiction, UFO, underground-base, and political-conspiracy strands were first joined in dated artifacts.

The model repeatedly moves too quickly from:

```text
similar visual or narrative feature
        ↓
possible exposure
        ↓
documented influence
```

The project must preserve those as three different evidence states.

## 4. Substantive corrections and boundaries

### 4.1 Wells Selenites

The primary text supports:

- underground lunar civilization;
- insect-like morphology;
- no nose or ears;
- dull bulging eyes positioned laterally;
- specialized and highly variable bodies.

It does not support the exact uploaded phrase `large black eyes`.

This matters because changing lateral bulging eyes into large black eyes artificially increases resemblance to the later Grey template.

### 4.2 Hill / *Outer Limits*

The reported twelve-day sequence is a valid contamination hypothesis, not a demonstrated influence edge.

Required separations:

```text
1961 event and earliest statements
        ≠
Betty's dreams
        ≠
1964 hypnosis sessions
        ≠
artist reconstructions
        ≠
1966 book
        ≠
1975 television dramatization
```

The decisive question is not whether an episode aired. It is whether Barney encountered it or whether the shared feature can be shown to have entered his report after exposure.

### 4.3 Marjorie Fish / Zeta Reticuli

The UNH finding aid confirms Fish correspondence, star charts, and later Zeta Reticuli debate material. It does not support collapsing the 1961 observation, Betty's sketch, Fish's three-dimensional model, and 1970s publication debate into one record.

### 4.4 Doreal

The acquisition combines several uncertain objects:

- *Mysteries of the Gobi*;
- *Emerald Tablets of Thoth the Atlantean*;
- uncertain 1940s/early-1950s dates;
- a serpent-race passage;
- a proposed Howard influence;
- a proposed Icke uptake.

Each requires its own artifact record. A later scholarly statement such as `in all likelihood` remains an interpretation unless supported by page-level comparison and exposure evidence.

### 4.5 *The X-Files*

A series-level row can document the franchise as a carrier, but specific mythology must be dated to its first episode or publication. The premiere date cannot be used as the date for concepts introduced later.

### 4.6 Dulce hierarchy

The statement that late-1980s Lear/Branton material contains the earliest documented hierarchy:

```text
Draconians / reptilians senior
Greys subordinate or bio-engineered servants
```

is the package's most important lead and its least adequately sourced major claim.

It requires:

- exact dated tape, transcript, newsletter, chapter, or pamphlet;
- creator and distributor;
- page/time locator;
- exact terminology;
- evidence distinguishing Lear, Cooper, Bennewitz, Doty, Moore, Walton/Branton, and later compilations;
- earlier-source search.

No canonical Grey/Reptilian fusion edge is created yet.

## 5. Harm and political-overlap boundary

Ancient serpent traditions must remain separate from modern claims about biological species or real human groups.

Modern reptilian-control narratives can reuse:

- bloodline conspiracy structures;
- Illuminati narratives;
- colonial pseudohistory;
- antisemitic tropes;
- claims targeting named families or populations.

The project may document those transformations and harms. It must not reproduce unsupported accusations as factual descriptions of living people or groups.
