## 1. Main finding

The best new textual witness states that Greys and Reptoids are `in league with each other`, while also describing a separate Reptilian Race as the Greys' enemy.

That relationship is:

```text

allied but tense

opponents / enemies
```

```text

        ↓ commands or creates
subordinate Greys
```

Therefore the reported 1989 Bishop text does not satisfy `FULL_LINKED_CHAIN`.
