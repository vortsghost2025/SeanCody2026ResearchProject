## 2. Decisive A/B/C correction

```text
A = reptilian or Draconian authority
B = Greys subordinate, created, controlled, or serving
C = underground base, treaty, genetic experiment, or secret program
```

### Presence is not subordination

Several model records incorrectly counted:

- Greys being present;
- multiple Grey types;
- Greys working with humans;
- Greys creating drones;

as B.

They do not satisfy B. B requires an explicit relation placing Greys beneath, under the control of, created by, or serving a distinct authority.

### Adjectival `reptilian` is not A

A phrase such as `reptilian skin` describing a Grey does not establish:

- a separate reptilian species;
- a Draconian ruling class;
- authority over Greys.

It may be a morphology adjective only.
