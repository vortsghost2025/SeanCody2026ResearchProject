### C. Palmer / Bernard / Byrd / Highjump cluster

The variants converge on a twentieth-century synthesis cluster but currently combine at least three different hypotheses:

1. **Palmer → Bernard:** textual quotation, shared mail-order/publishing infrastructure, or editorial synthesis.

These must remain split. An author invoking Byrd does not prove use of Operation Highjump records, and invoking Highjump does not prove access to National Archives files.
