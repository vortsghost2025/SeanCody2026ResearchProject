## Release policy

A candidate may leave quarantine only when the missing primary source or exact passage is acquired, the relevant truth layer is updated, and the record validates against schema v1.2.

Negative-evidence records may be canonical even when the underlying extraordinary claim is E-class. The negative conclusion itself must still be supported by a documented search scope and traceable sources.
