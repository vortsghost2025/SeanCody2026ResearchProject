This negative record must be revised when one pre-1990 artifact is acquired with:

1. exact date and date basis;
2. primary scan, recording or authenticated transcript;
3. page, frame or timestamp;
4. explicit same-referent A→B→C wording;
5. chain of custody or archive provenance;
6. edition/version identity;
7. evidence that later language was not inserted or rewritten by a compiler.
