## Why it matters

This text is one of the most frequently cited late-1980s Dulce items. Misclassifying it as a linked hierarchy artificially moves the mature Draco-over-Grey model backward in time.

The source instead preserves at least two relationship types:

```text
Greys ↔ Reptoids
alliance with tension

Greys ↔ Reptilian Race
enmity / opposition
```

Neither relationship is:

```text
Reptilian authority
        ↓ commands, creates or controls
Greys
```
