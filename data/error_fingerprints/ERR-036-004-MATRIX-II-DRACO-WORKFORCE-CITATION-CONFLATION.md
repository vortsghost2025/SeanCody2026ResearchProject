## Why this matters

```text
Matrix II primary wording:
Reptilians ↔ enemies of Greys

later attribution:
Draco → controls Greys
```

Those are different relationship structures.

A later parenthetical citation cannot convert one into the other.

## Required resolution

1. acquire the reported 1990 first edition;
2. acquire the exact later list or source making the Draco-workforce claim;
3. identify its page citation;
4. compare first edition, 1991 third edition and later editions;
5. determine whether the sentence was added, removed, miscited or imported from another writer;
6. record the earliest exact appearance of the Draco-workforce wording.

## Current use rule

Do not write:

> Matrix II says the Greys are the workforce of the Draco.

Allowed:

> Later sources attribute a Draco-workforce hierarchy to Matrix II, but the inspected 1991 third-edition text instead separates a higher culture that created Greys from Reptilian humanoids described as their enemies. The attribution remains unresolved pending exact-page and edition comparison.
