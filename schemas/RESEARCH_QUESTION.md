# Canonical Research Question Schema

Unresolved questions are first-class objects, not hidden notes.

**Location:** `research_questions/`  
**Naming:** `RQ-###`  
**Statuses:** `OPEN`, `SEARCHING`, `PARTIAL`, `RESOLVED`, `CLOSED_NEGATIVE`

```yaml
RQ:
  id: RQ-017
  title: Did Richard Doty or any AFOSI officer know the Shaver Mystery?
  status: SEARCHING
  priority: HIGH
  why_it_matters: >
    Confirmation could change the Shaver-to-Dulce relationship from motif
    similarity to intentional reuse or documented influence.
  related_entities:
    - ENTITY-Doty-001
    - ENTITY-Bennewitz-001
    - ENTITY-AFOSI-001
    - ENTITY-Shaver-001
    - ENTITY-Palmer-001
  related_records:
    - SHAVER-004
  search_targets:
    - Doty, Moore, and Bennewitz correspondence and reading history
    - AFOSI or Kirtland records
    - William Moore's 1989 MUFON statement
    - Greg Bishop, Project Beta
  what_would_resolve_it:
    positive: A document explicitly referencing Shaver or Palmer as source or template
    negative: A documented, sufficiently broad search finding no reference
    partial: Exposure evidence without specific citation
  current_evidence: UNKNOWN
  generated_session: SESSION_022
  last_updated: 2026-07-13
  assigned_to: UNASSIGNED
```
