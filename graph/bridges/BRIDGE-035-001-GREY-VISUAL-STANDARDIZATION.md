## Known corrections

### Wells 1901

The primary *First Men in the Moon* text does not describe `large black eyes`.

It describes insect-like Selenites with:

- no nose;
- no ears;
- dull bulging eyes at the side;
- highly specialized and variable body forms.

That is a legitimate comparison artifact but a weaker visual match than the LM Arena row claimed.

### Hill chronology

### Media artifacts

A series or film is an authenticated artifact. Its influence on a witness requires exposure evidence or distinctive post-exposure transfer.

## Evidence for

- broad visual elements recur across fiction, testimony, and media;
- mass-distributed images can supply common vocabulary and proportions;
- later Grey imagery becomes more standardized than early UFO humanoid descriptions;
- exact event, hypnosis, publication, and broadcast dates can be compared.

## Evidence against

- large heads, reduced faces, and large eyes may arise independently from neoteny, insect imagery, medical imagery, or generic alien design;
- witnesses may not have seen the proposed source;
- later summaries may retroactively normalize diverse original descriptions;
- some encounter reports remain unlike the standardized Grey after major media exposure.

## Promotion tests

1. Acquire exact Hill pre-hypnosis statements and each hypnosis transcript.
2. Acquire the relevant *Outer Limits* episode and document exact visual features.
3. Establish or fail to establish Barney Hill's exposure.
4. Build item-level records for 1975, 1977, and 1987 visual carriers.
5. Compare later witness statements only from earliest preserved versions.
6. Use Flatwoods and Pascagoula as negative controls for diversity.
7. Record which features appear, disappear, or become standardized at each stage.
