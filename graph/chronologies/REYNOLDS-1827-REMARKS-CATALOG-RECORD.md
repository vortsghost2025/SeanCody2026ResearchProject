## Why this artifact matters

The object postdates:

- the reported Reynolds–Symmes lecture-circuit break around 1825;
- John Quincy Adams's November 4, 1826 statement that Reynolds had `varied his purpose` toward Southern Ocean exploration.

Its existence proves that Reynolds remained publicly engaged with a printed review of Symmes's theory in 1827. It complicates any chronology in which all Symmes-related public activity ends immediately at the pivot.
