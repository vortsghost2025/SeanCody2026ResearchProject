## Initial distinction

```text
Could a vessel see a giant navigable opening?
        → potentially testable if it reached the correct region

Could it map beneath thick ice?
        → generally not with nineteenth-century shipboard methods

Could it infer the planet's internal structure?
        → not decisively from voyage observation alone
```

These statements are research framing, not conclusions about any opening or cavity.

## Entity guardrail

```text
Jeremiah N. Reynolds
        = advocate, writer, private-expedition organizer

William Reynolds
        = U.S. Navy midshipman, Wilkes Expedition observer
```

Any sighting, coordinate, or journal entry must be assigned to the correct person before interpretation.
